# Useful creative briefs

These prompts request supported methods; they do not assume assets or permissions that the user lacks. Ask the agent to inspect actual capabilities and ready sources first. Export only when the brief explicitly authorizes it.

## Editorial announcement

“Use the Clipzu skills to plan a six-second independent-film announcement. Establish an editorial direction, a headline/supporting type hierarchy and a limited warm/ink palette from my uploaded art. Describe the direction before editing. Keep the headline readable, stagger the supporting material, develop one second emphasis after the entrance, and end with a composed hold. Use separate image layers and supported numeric keyframes. If suitable title artwork is missing, explain the missing source rather than inventing a native text tool. Save and read back; show the actual preview when available.”

## Rhythm with sound

“Use my owned music clip and supplied beat markers to create an eight-second title study. Explain how the source trim maps beats to project time at this project's FPS. Use controlled movement, a readable pause and a scene handoff; not every layer should move at once. Do not invent audio analysis, ducking or audio-envelope tools. Preserve the original project outside the intended edit. Request one ordinary-plan MP4 after checking export gaps, then inspect its actual frames and audio timing.”

## Flat layered depth

“Plan a short product reveal with three owned transparent artwork layers. Let the foreground travel a little farther than the background and use restrained scale for depth. Call this a flat layered treatment; do not introduce a 3D camera, perspective, blur or a different renderer. Explain the hero, alignment and motion envelope before editing. Check interior and overlap frames so no moving layer covers the title unintentionally.”

## Existing project repair

“Read my chosen Clipzu project and identify why the middle feels static. Preserve its approved entrance, other clips and brand artwork. Propose one meaningful development beat and a controlled handoff using available transforms. Inspect existing keyframes before replacing a property track. If the required track is offset or has an unsupported curve, explain that boundary without flattening it. Save only the agreed correction and report the actual revision; do not render until requested.”

## Review before another render

“Review the saved project and the available MP4 against this brief. Separate technical failures, visible design issues and checks you cannot perform. Give time-specific feedback on hierarchy, reading duration, motion rhythm, overlap contrast and sound. Do not call a completed job creatively approved, and do not spend another export allowance without a concrete correction and authorization.”
